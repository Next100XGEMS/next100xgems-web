# Advertising and Commercial System

## Separation

Advertising is paid placement inventory. It is distinct from Featured Partners and Work With Us services. A sponsor may purchase visibility around data, never the data conclusion itself.

## Formats

1. Native Sponsored Cards
2. Contextual Display Placements
3. Section Sponsorships such as “Radar presented by PROJECT”

Every public ad must visibly say Sponsored. Ads must never pretend to be Radar signals or independent research.

## Conceptual inventory

Homepage: HP-01 Premium Spotlight, HP-02 Mid-page Native, HP-03 Research Sponsor. Research: RS-01 Article Header, RS-02 Inline Native, RS-03 Sidebar. Radar: RD-01 Radar Native, RD-02 Category Sponsor. Partners: PT-01 Featured Partner, PT-02 Partner Directory.

Future inventory may support campaign, sponsor, creative, placement, status, dates, device enablement, CTA, destination URL, impressions, unique views, clicks, and CTR. Provider and implementation choices are TBD.

## Commercial UX

No arrival popups, full-screen interstitials, autoplay sound, deceptive buttons, fake download buttons, flashing token ads, or undisclosed placements. Use restrained density, fewer mobile placements, and normally no more than approximately two significant homepage placements at once.

Advertise may eventually offer Request Media Kit and Book Campaign, with homepage, research, Radar, Featured Partners, and social inventory. Work With Us may sell Sponsored Content, X/Telegram campaigns, AMAs, launch campaigns, content production, and multi-platform distribution through campaign requests or consultations.

Featured Partners are paid placements and cannot influence Radar or editorial conclusions. Sponsor quality review should reject phishing, drainers, impersonation, fake presales, guaranteed-return claims, misleading yield claims, and obvious pump-and-dump creative.
