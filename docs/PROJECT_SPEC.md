# NEXT100XGEMS Project Specification

## Product

NEXT100XGEMS is a premium Crypto Intelligence + Crypto Media platform. Its product boundary is:

- Radar identifies market activity, signals, opportunities, and risks.
- Research explains and contextualizes evidence.
- Advertising promotes disclosed paid placements.
- Partnerships collaborate through distinct commercial relationships.

It is not a generic influencer landing page, a guaranteed “next 100x” service, an automated trading platform, a custody product, or an auto-buy/auto-sell system.

## Audiences

- B2C: crypto investors, traders, researchers, and followers.
- B2B: crypto projects, teams, sponsors, advertisers, and partners.

## Public information architecture

Planned public areas are Home, Radar, Research, Partners, Advertise, Network, Work With Us, Methodology, and Disclosure/Disclaimer.

Preferred navigation: Radar, Research, Partners, Advertise, Network. The prominent CTA is Work With Us.

Potential routes are `/`, `/radar`, `/radar/[token]`, `/research`, `/research/[slug]`, `/partners`, `/partners/[slug]`, `/advertise`, `/network`, `/work-with-us`, `/methodology`, `/disclosures`, and `/admin`. These are documentation only and must not be implemented in Phase 0.

## Homepage direction

The eventual hierarchy is Hero; trust/network proof; Radar preview; How Radar Works; Latest Research; Market Intelligence; Featured Partners; Work With Us; Case Studies; Social Network; Methodology/Transparency; Newsletter/Alerts; Footer.

Positioning direction: “Crypto moves fast. Your intelligence should move faster.” Copy is provisional. Primary CTAs are Explore Radar and Work With Us.

## Product boundaries

Radar must not promise returns, guarantee performance, recommend buying automatically, execute trades, or permit sponsors to purchase rankings, scores, risk ratings, trending status, or independent research conclusions. The separate token intelligence system remains WATCH/intelligence-only.

Research must clearly classify Editorial, Sponsored, Partner, and AI-assisted content. Sponsored content must not impersonate independent research.

Featured Partners are paid commercial placements distinct from advertising, editorial coverage, and Radar results. Public ads must visibly say Sponsored.

## Phase 0 status

This document specifies direction and boundaries only. No routes, integrations, schemas, admin controls, or product features are implemented in Phase 0.
